## 1. Highest-leverage single hire right now

### **Community & Content Lead** (already drafted in `HIRING_COMMUNITY_LEAD.md`)

**Why this and not engineering:**

Engineering is currently the **least** constrained function. Ido shipped Phase A in hours, not weeks. The work that's slowing us down is not code — it's the human-scale operations Asaf is doing alone:

| Function | Current state | Real bottleneck? |
|---|---|---|
| Code | Ido ships fast | No |
| Methodology / market analysis | Asaf is the source | No (this is the moat — should not be delegated) |
| Community operations | Ad-hoc, broadcast-only | **Yes** |
| Content publishing rhythm | Inconsistent | **Yes** |
| Member onboarding | Ad-hoc per member | **Yes** |
| Retention monitoring | None | **Yes** |

Four of six functions are blocked at the same place: Asaf is the founder, the methodology, the voice, AND the operator. The single highest-leverage move is to remove the last role from his plate.

### Why not Full-Stack Engineer instead

Three reasons:
1. **Ido is sufficient for current product velocity.** Phase A shipped in one weekend. Phase B is more data analysis than novel infrastructure.
2. **The differentiation is methodology + community, not code.** Adding a second engineer accelerates the wrong axis at this stage.
3. **A second engineer doubles coordination overhead immediately.** Ido alone is faster than Ido + 1 junior engineer for the next 4-6 months.

When code becomes the constraint (likely month 6-9, after Phase B + Stripe migration + tax form), that's when engineering hire #1 makes sense.

### Why not Sales/Marketing hire

The "marketing" function in our category is community + content. Hiring a marketer separately from a community lead splits the function in half and creates exactly the generic-marketing-fluff voice we are positioned against. The Community & Content Lead does both — they are the marketing function.

## 2. First 90 days — what we confirm to know it's the right hire

Five concrete outcomes. Miss any one of these → it's the wrong person.

| Outcome | Day to measure | What "right hire" looks like | What "wrong hire" looks like |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Editorial cadence shipping 5x/wk across 3+ channels** from Asaf's raw inputs | Day 30 | Asaf records 1 hour, you produce X thread + Substack post + YouTube short + WhatsApp recap | Asaf still writing his own posts at Day 30 |
| **First-14-day onboarding flow built and live** for all new paying members | Day 30 | Documented sequence: welcome, Day 3 check-in, Day 7 first-trade nudge, Day 14 retention call. ≥60% activation by Day 14. | "Working on it" still at Day 30 |
| **WhatsApp engagement +50% replies/post** vs Day 0 baseline | Day 60 | Member health dashboard live, top-10 members identified and engaged with personally, broadcast → 2-way conversation shift | Engagement unchanged — they're posting but not building relationships |
| **Weekly Market Read fully operationalized** — they own everything except Asaf's actual presentation | Day 60 | Registration page, day-of reminders, recording, recap email, no-show follow-up all done by them | Asaf still doing the recap email at Day 60 |
| **Asaf's time on content production drops to ≤1 hr/wk** without quality dropping | Day 90 | Asaf can take a 2-week vacation and content keeps shipping | Asaf still in the daily loop at Day 90 |

If 4 of 5 hit by Day 90 → right hire, keep them, push toward 12-month outcomes.
If 2-3 of 5 hit → coaching conversation, set 30-day improvement plan.
If 0-1 hit → it's the wrong person, exit clean before Day 120 protected period.

## 3. Ideal candidate profile

### Experience (must have)
- Has run an online community of 500+ engaged members. Real engagement metrics, not page-likes.
- Has built and maintained editorial calendars across 3+ channels with documented results.
- Has written under someone else's name (ghostwriting / founder voice extension).
- Native Hebrew. Near-native English (methodology source material is English).
- Can read a stock chart well enough to understand what a member is asking ("VCP forming on NVDA, volume drying").

### Working style (must have)
- **Operates without briefs.** They write the brief. Ido reviews, doesn't dictate.
- **Ships small and often.** Day 5 publishes something publicly, not Day 30.
- **Owns metrics, not vibes.** Reports numbers, not "engagement felt good this week."
- **Direct communication.** No phrase-softening, no "circle back," no calendar Tetris.

### Mindset (must have)
- Treats the methodology with rigor. Doesn't try to "humanize" it into hype.
- Views the community as their primary product, not as an audience to extract from.
- Is comfortable being publicly visible. They will become "voice 2" within 6 months — if that idea makes them anxious, wrong person.
- Reads Howard Marks memos and IBD case studies for fun. Not because they have to.

### Cultural fit gates (any one of these = exit interview)
- Uses "rock star" / "passionate" / "exciting opportunity" unironically
- Refers to traders as "users" or "consumers" — they're traders, not eyeballs
- Wants to "run our brand strategy" or "rethink our voice"
- Anxious about ambiguity. Cannot start without org chart and weekly 1:1 cadence
- Treats financial markets as gambling or scam

## 4. Risk of hiring now vs waiting 6 months

### Hiring now (Day 0)
**Upside if right:**
- Asaf's time recovers immediately for the only work that compounds (methodology depth, weekly Market Read, premium-tier 1:1s)
- Community engagement engine starts at 100 members instead of 500 — easier to systematize when small
- Day-90 metrics establish operating cadence before Phase B + tax 1322 + Stripe load up Ido's plate
- Salary cost: ~18k NIS/mo × 6 months = 108k NIS. At 500 subs × $59 avg, that's recoverable in <2 months of MRR contribution.

**Downside if wrong:**
- 90 days of misalignment + ~4 months total runway loss to exit clean = ~70k NIS sunk cost
- Cultural drag during the period: a wrong community hire can degrade the community quality in ways that take months to repair
- Mitigation: rigorous Day-30/Day-60/Day-90 gating per Section 2

### Waiting 6 months
**Upside:**
- More MRR to confirm budget
- More users to validate hiring against (clearer role definition)
- Risk-free zero spend

**Downside (the bigger risk):**
- Asaf-bottleneck compounds. At 500-800 members, manual community operations break and retention drops
- Hiring at the broken state is harder — new hire walks into a fire instead of building a system from baseline
- 6-month delay = 6 months of suboptimal content cadence = ~$30k-$60k of compounding lost MRR (founder content discipline correlates directly with subscriber growth at this stage)
- Engineering bottleneck shifts to engineering at exactly the wrong time (Phase B, Stripe migration, tax 1322 all stacking)

### Recommendation
**Hire now.** The asymmetric bet is to hire while the system is small and shapeable, not after the system breaks. Bad-hire downside is bounded (~70k NIS, 4 months); good-hire upside is the difference between 500 paid subscribers in 6 months and 1,500 in 12.

## 5. Contractor / Fractional / Agency — when each makes sense

| Model | Cost / mo | When this is the right answer | When it isn't |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Full-time hire** | 15-25k NIS | The role is a permanent function (community ops, dedicated voice extension, daily ownership). | The role is a one-time project, or scope is fuzzy. |
| **Part-time fractional (2-3 days/wk)** | 8-12k NIS | You're testing whether the role is actually needed before committing. Or you can't afford full-time yet. | The role requires daily presence (community moderation, real-time engagement). |
| **Contractor / freelance** | 4-10k NIS depending on scope | Defined deliverable: build the editorial calendar template + first 30 days of content. Done. | Ongoing relationship-management work (community moderation, member 1:1s). |
| **Agency (content / community)** | 15-40k NIS | Heavy production work (video editing, podcast post-production, design). Specialized. | Voice / authenticity work. An agency cannot extend Asaf's voice — it will sound corporate. |

### Specific recommendation for THIS role
**Full-time hire.** Reasoning:

1. The role's primary value comes from **continuity of relationships** — knowing each top-10 member's history, voice, preferences. A fractional hire breaks this on Day 1.
2. The voice-extension work requires **daily proximity to Asaf**, not weekly check-ins.
3. The cost math works at 100 paying subscribers ($1,900 MRR projected at current pricing, ~$5,900 at new tier mix). Even on conservative numbers, the salary is <50% of attributable MRR contribution by month 6.

### Where a contractor IS the right move now
**Tactical content production support.** Examples:
- Video editor for repurposing weekly Market Read recordings into YouTube + Instagram reels (5 hrs/wk, ~3k NIS/mo)
- Designer for Substack and X visual asset templates (one-time, ~4k NIS)
- Israeli tax expert consultation for form 1322 export logic (one-time, ~2k NIS)

These are bounded, technical, and don't require relationship continuity. Hire contractors for these in month 2-3 after the Community & Content Lead is in seat.

### Where an agency makes sense (later, month 6+)
- Video production for a "year-1 results" public report (one-off, ~10-20k NIS)
- Performance ad management once we start paid acquisition (~$1k-$3k/mo, % of spend)

Until then, no agency. Agencies dilute voice and double our coordination overhead at our scale.

## 6. Prioritized hiring roadmap — next 3 hires in sequence

### Hire 1 (Month 0): **Community & Content Lead** — full-time
- Cost: 15-22k NIS/mo + 0.25-0.5% equity, 4-year vest, 1-year cliff
- Reasoning: Unblocks Asaf, the single hardest constraint on the system. Detailed in `HIRING_COMMUNITY_LEAD.md`.
- Success criteria: Section 2 above.
- Risk if delayed: founder burnout, content cadence collapse, retention drop at scale.

### Hire 2 (Month 4-6): **Full-Stack Engineer** — full-time or senior contractor
- Cost: 25-35k NIS/mo full-time, or ~15k NIS/mo for senior contractor 3 days/wk
- Reasoning: By month 4-6, Ido's bottleneck shifts from "I have nothing to ship" to "I have 4 things to ship and 1 brain." Triggers: Phase B done, Stripe migration done, tax 1322 done — and now we want broker integrations + AI coaching layer + mobile app.
- Success criteria:
  - Phase C features (Confidence Score, Position Lifecycle Alerts) ship in their first quarter
  - PR review cycle reduces Ido's IC time by 40%+
  - Zero increase in production incidents
- Why not now: doubles team coordination overhead without enough engineering work in the queue to justify. Wait until the queue is real.
- Profile: Senior backend or full-stack. Strong TypeScript + Postgres. Calm under shipping pressure. Israeli or remote-friendly TZ.
- Risk if hired too early: Ido spends 30% of his time managing instead of shipping. Net velocity drops.

### Hire 3 (Month 7-9): **Customer Success / Member Operations** — full-time or senior contractor
- Cost: 12-18k NIS/mo
- Reasoning: At ~800-1,000 paying subscribers, the Community & Content Lead can no longer also do welcome calls, churn-risk interventions, billing support, and tier-upgrade conversations. The CS role takes the operational load so the Community Lead can stay strategic.
- Success criteria:
  - <12-hour first-response time on all support
  - Manage the in-app referral program operationally
  - Drive ≥30% of tier upgrades through 1:1 outreach
  - Surface churn-risk signals weekly to Asaf + Community Lead
- Profile: Operations-minded, Hebrew + English, comfortable with a shared inbox + Notion + PostHog
- Why not earlier: at <500 subs, the Community & Content Lead can handle 1:1 work themselves. Hiring CS earlier creates an idle role.

### Hires explicitly NOT in the next 12 months
- **Sales/BD lead** — we are self-serve subscription. No sales motion. Hiring this role at our stage = inventing a function that doesn't fit the business model.
- **Marketing manager (generic)** — content + community IS our marketing. A generalist marketer would duplicate and dilute.
- **Designer (full-time)** — current product UI is shippable; design needs are contractor-scale until at least month 12.
- **Head of X** — we are too small to have heads of anything. Premature title structure creates fake hierarchy and slows decisions.
