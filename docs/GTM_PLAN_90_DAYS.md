# Momentum Playbook — 90-Day Go-to-Market Plan

> Operator's playbook for taking Momentum Playbook from warm-audience beta to first 500–1,000 paying subscribers.
> Author: Head of Growth perspective · Team of 2 (Asaf = founder/voice, ido = product/eng)

---

## Strategic Frame

### What we are
A **professional-grade, methodology-strict** momentum trading platform + community. CAN SLIM / Minervini Trend Template / VCP / AVWAP — operationalized as software, not opinion.

### What we are NOT
- Not a stock-alert Discord
- Not a hype channel
- Not a generalist trading journal (we are zero-compromise Stage 2)

### The wedge
There are 1,000+ generic stock alert servers. There are 0 platforms where the methodology is *enforced* by the software. That's our wedge — and the marketing follows.

### Hard constraints
- Bootstrapped (no paid acquisition budget for 60 days)
- Team of 2 (every hour matters)
- Hebrew-first audience, but methodology travels globally — bilingual content unlocks scale
- Subscription economics → retention >>> raw acquisition

### Strategic priorities (in order)
1. **Activate the warm audience.** The existing WhatsApp community is the single highest-leverage asset. Treat it as a launch channel, not a marketing channel.
2. **Build a content moat.** We cannot outspend competitors. We will out-publish them with data, not opinion.
3. **Engineer for retention before scale.** A 30-day churn problem at 100 subs is a death spiral at 1,000.
4. **Founder-led distribution.** Asaf is the voice. Every interview, post, and webinar should be him until day 60.

# Phase 1 — Pre-launch (Days 1–30)

## Goals
- Validate willingness-to-pay with warm cohort
- Recruit 10 design partners actively using the product
- Build content backlog + publishing rhythm
- Build pre-launch waitlist of 200+
- Lock measurement infrastructure (no decisions without data)

## North-Star Metric for Phase 1
**200+ qualified waitlist sign-ups AND 10 design partners with ≥3 trades logged each.**
A "qualified" sign-up answers the onboarding survey honestly: trades momentum stocks ≥ once/month, has account ≥ $5k, prior experience with one of CAN SLIM / Minervini / VCP.

## Channel Reasoning (Phase 1)

**Why WhatsApp first:** Highest-trust audience we already have. Conversion rate of 5-15% is realistic from warm community. Cold paid would be 0.5-2%.

**Why X over LinkedIn:** Active US momentum traders live on FinTwit. LinkedIn is for B2B SaaS. Wrong audience.

**Why Substack:** Owned distribution. Email cannot be deplatformed, downranked, or paywalled by an algorithm change.

**Why design partners over open beta:** 10 engaged users producing testimonials > 100 disengaged users producing churn. Quality of feedback drives product fit.

**Why no paid ads:** Bootstrapped + unproven funnel = paid spend is gambling. We need organic channels working first to know what to amplify.

# Phase 2 — Launch Window (Days 31–45)

## Goals
- Convert waitlist to first **100 paying subscribers**
- Generate 3 high-leverage content artifacts (case study, demo video, methodology guide)
- Establish daily content rhythm at scale
- Surface and fix top-3 activation friction points

## North-Star Metric for Phase 2
**100 paying subscribers, $1,900+ MRR, and ≥ 60% Day-7 retention on new sign-ups.**
Retention > 60% Day-7 = product is sticky enough to scale. Below that = fix product before pouring more leads in.

## Channel Reasoning (Phase 2)

**Why founding-member pricing:** Scarcity (capped at 100) + permanence (lifetime rate) drives immediate action. Standard playbook for early-stage products with constrained CAC budget.

**Why personal WhatsApp message over polished campaign:** The community trusts Asaf as a person. The moment it sounds like marketing, conversion drops. Authenticity is a hard moat at this stage.

**Why founder-led welcome calls:** They convert undecided "I think it's cool" → "I'm telling my friends about this". Plus they surface product issues from real money customers. Brutal but invaluable.

**Why in-app referral now (not later):** Referral loops compound. Every day you delay implementing one is a day you lose virality from the most enthusiastic launch cohort.

**Why no Product Hunt / Hacker News yet:** US-centric, generalist audiences. Wrong fit until we have an English-language story to tell and the product to back it. Save the shot for Phase 3.

## Channel Reasoning (Phase 3)

**Why broker affiliates over influencer partnerships:** Brokers' customers ARE the ICP. Influencer audiences are noisier. Higher conversion, longer-term relationship.

**Why YouTube long-form over X scale-up:** X has reached saturation against the Israeli trader audience. YouTube is global, evergreen, and ranks in Google.

**Why paid only after Day 60:** Paid ads against unproven funnel = setting money on fire. Paid ads against a proven funnel = scalable lever. Sequence matters.

**Why tax form 1322 is a growth play (not just a feature):** Annual tax season creates an annual unmissable use-case. Israeli traders cannot avoid it. We become the only platform that handles it = annual reactivation event.

**Why public 90-day report:** Aggregate user data is the single most credible content asset we can produce. Whoop and Strava both use this playbook. The data sells the product.

# Cross-Phase Operating Principles

### Cadence rituals (don't break)
- **Daily:** X market read post (Asaf, 9:30 ET / 16:30 IL) + 1 PostHog metric check (ido)
- **Weekly:** Substack publish (Sundays), team review (Mondays 09:00), customer call (1× /week minimum)
- **Bi-weekly:** New onboarding cohort review, churn cohort review
- **Monthly:** Public progress report (transparency = retention)

### What we will refuse to do (anti-priorities)
- Generic stock alerts ("$NVDA looks good") — destroys positioning
- Sponsored hype content with influencers
- Lifetime deals (kills LTV math)
- Free tier with no trial cap (kills urgency)
- Paid ads in Phase 1 (premature optimization)
- Building features customers requested before validating with usage data

### Pricing playbook
- **Founding Member:** $9.50/mo lifetime, capped at first 100
- **Pro Monthly:** $19/mo (current)
- **Pro Annual:** $190/yr (2 months free) — launches Day 31
- **Pro Lifetime:** never offered. Kills retention math.

### What success looks like at Day 90
- 500+ paying subscribers
- $9,500+ MRR
- Month-2 retention ≥ 85%
- One profitable acquisition channel beyond warm community
- 12+ weeks of compounding daily content
- 1 public report establishing data credibility
- Asaf recognized as the methodology authority in Israeli momentum trading
