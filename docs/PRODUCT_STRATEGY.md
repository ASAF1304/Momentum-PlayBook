# Momentum Playbook — Product Differentiation Strategy

## The Thesis

**Differentiation is not the method. Differentiation is the method as enforced behavior.**

A book teaches a method. A trading journal *tracks* what you did.
Momentum Playbook *enforces* what Minervini taught — turning rules into active guardrails the trader cannot bypass.

The competition (TraderSync, Tradervue, Edgewonk) is generic across trading styles. Momentum Playbook is uncompromisingly built for one persona: the **Stage 2 momentum trader operating Minervini-style**.

The lock-in:
1. Trader spends 30 days using the app's structure
2. App accumulates personal edge data (which hours, days, setups make money)
3. App detects personal failure patterns (revenge, FOMO, oversizing)
4. Trader cannot trust themselves without the app's structure
5. Switching cost is the entire personal trading dataset

---

## The 3 Pillars

### Pillar 1 — Method Enforcement (BUILD FIRST)
The system actively prevents the trader from breaking Minervini's rules.

- **Pre-Trade Lock** — Cannot open Add Trade form without passing Validator first
- **Hard Blocks** — Stop distance >10% blocks save (Minervini rule)
- **Smart Warnings** — "78% of your Stage-4-market trades lost money. Continue?"
- **Cooling-Off Automation** — 2 consecutive losers this week → 24h cooldown on new entries
- **Monthly Loss Limit Active** — when hit, app locks new entries until next month
- **Earnings Block** — auto-block entries within 5 trading days of ER (Minervini rule)
- **Stage 4 Market Mode** — when SPY breaks 30-week MA, position sizer auto-reduces to 50%

### Pillar 2 — Personal Edge Discovery
The app surfaces the trader's unique edge from their own data.

- **Edge Heatmap** — visual: by hour, day-of-week, setup, market regime → where YOU make money
- **Best Setup Report** — every trader has 1-2 high-edge setups. The app identifies yours.
- **"Stop doing this" Alerts** — anti-pattern detection from your own history
- **Confidence vs. Outcome Plot** — usually inverse for amateurs (high confidence = revenge)
- **Stop-Move Tracker** — every stop move logged. "You moved stops down 12 times in March."

### Pillar 3 — Daily Accountability Ritual
A morning prep and evening review that becomes the trader's anchor.

- **Morning Prep (9:00 ET)** — mandatory checklist:
  - Market Stage assessment (2/3/4)
  - Max daily risk %
  - Open positions review
  - Mental state self-check (yes/no)
- **Evening Review (17:00 ET)** — what did you learn? best trade? worst decision?
- **Streak Counter** — consecutive days completing the ritual. Resets on miss.
- **Weekly AI Coaching Report** — Sunday: AI-generated review of your week

## Extended Feature Ideas (Beyond the 3 Pillars)

### Identity & Stickiness
- **The "Abllin Method"** — name the methodology after Asaf. Branded identity creates loyalty (Wyckoff, Minervini, Weinstein → Abllin).
- **The Asaf Lens** — weekly market read, his commentary on every Stage 2 Leader, his picks. Subscribers feel they have a mentor.
- **Anonymous Cohort Leaderboard** — "your win rate is in top 22% this month" — social proof + dopamine.
- **Mentor Mode for new traders** — guided first 10 trades with explanations.

### Behavioral Engineering
- **Trade Confidence Score** — rate 1-10 before entry. Surface correlation later.
- **Pre-trade Cooldown Timer** — 60-second wait + checklist before Add Trade form opens. Kills FOMO.
- **Trade Voice Memos** — 30-sec audio per trade: "why am I entering this?" — playback creates accountability.
- **Watch-and-Wait Timer** — when Validator blocks, start 24h timer. Track shadow performance of blocked trades. Builds discipline data.
- **Confidence-Adjusted Sizer** — sloppy logging (no notes/screenshot) → auto-reduce position to 50%.

### Risk Intelligence
- **Real-time Portfolio Risk** — "if all stops hit today, you lose $X (Y% of account)".
- **Risk-Budget System** — daily risk depletes like an energy bar.
- **Market Regime Detection** — automatic Stage 2/3/4 detection of SPY/QQQ at the top of the app, with DEFEND MODE banner in Stage 3-4.

### Trade Lifecycle Management
- **Position Lifecycle Alerts**:
  - 1R hit → "take 1/3" reminder
  - 8 weeks holding → review badge (Minervini's 8-week rule)
  - Below 50-day MA → exit signal
  - Volume drying up → caution flag
- **Stage Transition Alerts** — push notification when held stock breaks 30-week MA.

### Memory & Pattern Recognition
- **Setup Library** — visual archive of every winning VCP/breakout you took, tagged.
- **Trade Replay / Time Machine** — chart snapshot at entry. Replay your decision moment.
- **Failed-trade Postmortem Templates** — structured retrospective per loser.

### Integrations / Killer Features
- **Multi-broker execution** — Sizer → eToro/IBKR/IBI API → one-click execution. THE retention play.
- **Israeli Tax Reporting (טופס 1322)** — auto-generate Israeli capital gains report. הופך אותה לחובה לכל סוחר ישראלי.
- **Earnings Calendar integration** — block dates auto-flagged.
