# Community & Content Lead — Momentum Playbook

## Why this role exists, plainly

We have 300 active members in our WhatsApp community and a 90-day plan to triple it. Asaf is the founder, the methodology, and the voice — and he cannot keep being all three at the scale we're heading to. We need the person who turns Asaf's Sunday market read into the Tuesday DM that turns into the Friday upgrade.

This is not a marketing role. It's not a social media role. It's the person who extends one founder's voice across a community without diluting it, and who builds the engagement engine that makes people not want to cancel.

---

## What the role actually solves

Five problems are currently breaking us:

1. **The WhatsApp community is mostly one-way broadcast.** Asaf posts. People read. We don't know who's engaged, who's about to churn, or who's ready to buy the next tier. We need someone running 2-way activation.

2. **Content publishing is inconsistent because there's no system.** Asaf writes when he has time. There's no editorial cadence, no backlog, no repurposing across channels. One good X thread should become a Substack post, a YouTube short, and a recap email — today it dies in 12 hours.

3. **Onboarding for new paying members is ad-hoc.** Some get a personal welcome from Asaf. Most don't. The first 14 days predict 90% of churn. Right now we're winging it.

4. **We can't see who's about to leave.** No usage pulse, no NPS, no "this member hasn't opened the app in 10 days" alerts. By the time someone cancels, the relationship is already dead.

5. **The weekly Market Read session has no operations behind it.** Asaf currently shows up, hosts, and posts the recap himself. Every part of that runway should be done by you — show notes, calendar invites, the recording, the recap, the follow-up to people who registered but didn't attend.

If you read those and thought "I've fixed exactly this twice before" — keep reading.

## What you'll do in your first 90 days

### Days 1–30 — Learn the audience, fix the broken processes

- Read every WhatsApp message from the last 60 days. Tag the recurring questions, the engagement patterns, the silent members.
- Sit through 5 trades with Asaf (live or recorded). You don't need to become a trader, but you need to understand the difference between a VCP and a flat base, and why we never enter against Stage 4.
- Build the editorial calendar for the next 30 days from Asaf's existing content backlog.
- Define and ship the "first 14 days" onboarding flow for new paying members: welcome message, Day 3 check-in, Day 7 "have you logged your first trade", Day 14 retention call from Asaf if not activated.
- Set up a member health dashboard (we'll show you the data). At minimum: WhatsApp engagement, app login frequency, days since last trade logged.

**Success metric for Day 30:** Editorial cadence shipping at 5x/week on at least 3 channels. Day-14 onboarding flow live for all new members.

### Days 31–60 — Take ownership of the engagement engine

- You're now publishing across X, Substack, YouTube, and WhatsApp from Asaf's raw inputs (1 hour of his time → 4 channels of output).
- Run the operations behind every weekly Market Read session: registration page, day-of reminders, recording, recap, no-show follow-up.
- Identify the top 10 "high-signal" community members (the ones who reply, ask sharp questions, post their own setups). Build a private side-channel for them with Asaf.
- Surface 5 "at-risk" members per week to Asaf with context. He sends a personal note. You track the result.

**Success metric for Day 60:** Avg replies per WhatsApp post +50% vs Day 0. ≥ 60% Day-14 activation rate on new paying members.

### Days 61–90 — Build the systems that scale

- Document everything you've built: the editorial process, the onboarding flow, the health dashboard methodology. So a junior community ops hire in month 6 can take it over.
- Launch the first member referral campaign. Coordinate with Ido on the in-app referral mechanic. Drive 20+ tracked referrals.
- Run a quarterly content retrospective with Asaf: what got engagement, what didn't, where do we double down.
- Start being recognizable to the community. Members should know your name and DM you, not just Asaf.

**Success metric for Day 90:** 30%+ of new paying members coming via referral or community-driven sources. Asaf spending 1 hr/wk on content production (down from 5+) without quality dropping.
